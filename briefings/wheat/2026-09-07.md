KC HRW cash bid fell 4.4% on the week, from $9.30 to $8.89/bu, a bigger move than the recent trend — trailing 8-week volatility sits at 5.03%, so this week's drop is close to a one-week-stdev event on its own. Nothing in the headline set explains a KC-specific driver; the wheat news this week is either agronomic (sawfly biocontrol research) or policy noise out of Pakistan (FPCCI/farmer pricing complaints), none of which map cleanly onto US cash basis moves.

On export sales: net sales for the week ending 8/27 came in at 313,516 tonnes, essentially in line with the trailing 8-week average (308,991), a surprise of about +4,500 tonnes — negligible. So the pace of new bookings held steady even as KC cash fell hard. Worth flagging that the sales figure is all-wheat-classes/national, while the price move is a regional HRW cash bid, so don't read the flat sales pace as confirmation either way on the price drop; they're not describing the same wheat.

Watch item: next week's ESR release (covering the week the price actually fell) to see if the cash weakness starts showing up in slower export bookings, or if it's a basis/local phenomenon.
