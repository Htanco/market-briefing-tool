KC HRW cash bid fell to $8.54/bu, down 1.9% on the week from $8.71. Headlines flagging "continued weakness" and Thursday's move lower are consistent with the tape, though nothing in the news flow points to a specific domestic driver — this looks like it's tracking broader wheat sentiment rather than a KC-specific story. Trailing 8-week volatility sits at 4.34% weekly stdev, so a ~2% move is unremarkable, within normal noise for this market.

On the export side, national all-wheat-classes net sales for the week ending 9/17 came in at 267,553 tonnes, about 40,600 tonnes below the trailing 8-week average of ~308,000. Pace is soft but not collapsing. The Black Sea disruption headlines (Russia-Ukraine grain corridor tied to oil refinery strikes) are a genuine supply-side risk for global wheat flows, and Bangladesh shifting demand toward India for wheat is a demand-side data point, but neither maps cleanly onto this week's US export-sales miss — worth watching rather than concluding causation.

Watch: next week's ESR report for whether the sales-pace softness extends or was a one-week blip, especially if Black Sea flows tighten further.
