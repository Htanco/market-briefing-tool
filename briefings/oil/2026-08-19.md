WTI closed the week at $84.05/bbl, up $5.11 (+6.5%) from $78.94 prior week. That's a big move in the context of trailing 8-week vol of ~8%, so this week alone accounts for most of a typical volatility band – worth flagging as an outlier week rather than trend confirmation.

Inventory data is a bit of a puzzle against the price action: stocks rose 4.4mm bbls, well above the 8-week average build of ~774k bbls (surprise of +3.6mm bbls). Normally a build that far above trend would be bearish/price-dampening, yet price rallied sharply in the same week. That divergence suggests the price move is being driven by something outside the inventory print itself – possibly macro, geopolitical, or positioning-related – but with no headlines provided this week, we can't pin down the driver and shouldn't guess.

Net: price up sharply on a week with a larger-than-usual inventory build, which is an unusual combination worth flagging.

Watch item: check whether next week's price action gives back some of this gain, which would support the idea that this was a one-off spike rather than a fundamentals-driven repricing.
