Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-08-26 📈

WTI closed the week at $87.35, up $3.30 (+3.9%) from $84.05. That's a sizable weekly move, and it lines up with the elevated vol reading (8-week stdev of weekly % changes at 6.9%) — this market's been choppy, not trending smoothly.

Inventories rose 95k bbls, well below the 8-week average build of ~2.09mm bbls, a surprise of about -1.99mm bbls. So a much smaller build than the recent pace, which is directionally supportive of price even though it's still a build, not a draw.

Headlines are a bit of a mismatch with the price action — several note oil "dropping" and slipping on hopes the Strait of Hormuz could reopen, which would be bearish, not bullish. That suggests the sentiment in the headlines predates or contradicts this week's rally, so I wouldn't lean on them to explain the move. Enbridge's $600mm US oil asset purchase is a corporate/M&A story, not a macro driver. Net: price strength this week looks more attributable to the inventory undershoot than to the news flow.

Watch item: next week's inventory print, to see if the smaller build was noise or the start of a tightening trend.
