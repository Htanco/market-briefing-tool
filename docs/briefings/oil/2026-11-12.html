Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-11-12 📉

WTI closed the week at $105.59, down $1.58 (-1.47%) from $107.17. Draw continued for an 8th straight week - inventories fell 865k bbls to 409.76mm, though the draw was smaller than the trailing 8-week average (-1,729k), leaving a positive surprise of 864k bbls. In other words, stocks are still declining but at a decelerating pace, which is arguably consistent with price slipping rather than holding - less tightness than the recent run-rate implies. Vol remains elevated at 5.92% (8-week stdev of weekly returns), so the move isn't out of line with recent choppiness.

Headlines are mixed-to-neutral for direction: "no Iran war but no peace" and commentary on gas prices falling as "more oil flows" both point to ample supply keeping a lid on price, consistent with this week's pullback. Nothing here points to a demand shock or fresh supply disruption - more a continuation of the range-bound, oversupplied tone.

Watch: whether next week's inventory draw reaccelerates back toward the 8-week average pace, which would support price, or if the smaller draws persist and add pressure.
