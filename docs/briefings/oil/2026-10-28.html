Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-10-28 📈

WTI closed the week at $107.17, up $7.24 (+7.25%) from $99.93 last week — a sharp move, consistent with the elevated 6.04% trailing 8-week vol we've been seeing. Headlines are mixed and arguably offsetting: Iran mediation/diplomacy stories cut both ways (one has oil ticking up on mediation hopes, another has it declining on diplomacy signs), so it's hard to pin the weekly gain cleanly on geopolitics despite the Mideast backdrop (Saudi exports holding up despite Houthi attacks). UBS's bump to a $110 Brent target by end-2026 is directionally consistent with the move but is a forecast, not a cause. On inventories, stocks drew down 1,892 kb, a smaller draw than the recent 8-week average (-2,049 kb), leaving a positive surprise of +157 kb — i.e., inventories came in slightly looser than the recent trend, which doesn't support the price spike on fundamentals alone. Net: this week's rally looks more sentiment/geopolitics-driven than inventory-driven. Watch: next week's inventory print for confirmation of whether draws reaccelerate, which would validate the price move rather than leave it looking overextended.
