Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-11-19 📉

WTI fell hard this week, down $5.19 (-5.4%) to $90.78, a sharp move that lines up with the trailing 8-week vol reading of 6.3%, so this wasn't an outlier relative to recent choppiness, just a big print in one direction. Headlines point to supply-side easing as the driver — multiple wires citing "ample global supplies" and fading supply concerns — consistent with the price action, though we don't have a specific catalyst (OPEC+ comments, Russian flows, etc.) to pin it on.

Inventories added 3.38M bbls, a build against a trailing 8-week average draw of ~1.1M, so the surprise is a meaningful +4.5M swing versus recent trend. That build supports the "ample supply" narrative in the headlines and adds some fundamental backing to the price drop rather than it being purely sentiment-driven.

Other headlines (Qaiyarah tender, Kenya's Lokichar start-up) are more structural/longer-term supply additions, not likely to be moving price this week specifically, but worth flagging as part of the broader supply growth backdrop.

Watch: next week's inventory print for confirmation of a genuine trend change versus a one-off build.
