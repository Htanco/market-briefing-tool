Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-11-05 📉

WTI closed the week at $105.59, down $1.58 (-1.47%) from $107.17. Draw smaller than the recent trend - inventories fell 865k bbls vs an 8-week average draw of ~1,729k, so the build/draw surprise was +864k (less drawdown than expected), a modestly bearish inventory signal. Vol remains elevated at 5.92% stdev on weekly moves, so don't over-read a single-week fade.

Headline flow this week was mixed and partly macro-driven: reports of oil tumbling post-election alongside a stock rally suggest some of the weakness was broader risk-sentiment/macro rotation rather than oil-specific fundamentals. Separately, chatter around a potential quick resolution to the Iran conflict would also argue for pressure on crude from reduced geopolitical risk premium. On the supply side, Cenovus downplaying the wide Canadian crude discount as temporary, and Colombia's $10B restart plans, both point to incremental supply-side normalization/growth, consistent with the softer price action. None of these are confirmed drivers of this specific week's move - just corroborating context.

Watch: any follow-through on Iran de-escalation headlines next week, as that's the most likely swing factor for the geopolitical risk premium in WTI.
