Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-09-09 📉

WTI closed the week at $84.62, down $2.73 (-3.1%) from $87.35. That's a sizeable weekly drop, and it comes despite a draw in crude stocks — inventories fell 4.45mm bbls, a build-to-draw swing of roughly 7mm bbls versus the recent 8-week average trend. Normally a draw of that size would be price-supportive, so the divergence is worth flagging; it suggests demand or macro concerns are outweighing the tighter physical balance this week. Vol remains elevated at 6.76% (8-week stdev of weekly returns), consistent with a market still repricing headline risk.

Headlines are noisy and partly stale/inconsistent with the price action — several reference $100 oil and a US-Iran escalation, which don't square with this week's selloff to the mid-$80s, so we'd treat those as either old or unrelated to this print. The Enbridge-Tallgrass deal ($2.55bn) is more of a midstream consolidation story than a price driver. Given the inventory/price disconnect, don't overweight any single headline narrative here.

Watch: next week's inventory report to see if the draw was a one-off or the start of a trend, which would help explain whether price is lagging or leading fundamentals.
