Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-09-30 📉

WTI closed the week at $93.57/bbl, down $9.97 (-9.6%) from $103.54 prior week — a sharp reversal that sits oddly against the headline flow, which is mostly constructive (tight product markets, stalled Iran talks, strong Russian export earnings). None of the headlines point to a clear bearish catalyst of this size, so treat the move with caution — could be profit-taking after a run-up, macro/demand jitters, or a data point not captured here. Worth flagging to the team rather than explaining away.

Inventories built modestly, +922k bbls, but that's well below the trailing 8-week average build of ~2,736k — a negative surprise of about -1,814k, meaning the build was smaller than the recent pattern, which if anything leans supportive for crude, not bearish. That's another reason the price drop doesn't line up cleanly with fundamentals this week.

Realized vol is elevated at ~7.0% (stdev of weekly % moves, trailing 8 weeks), and this week's -9.6% print is consistent with that vol regime, not an outlier relative to recent choppiness.

Watch: confirm next week whether the drop extends or reverses — if it reverses quickly, this week looks like noise rather than a fundamental repricing.
