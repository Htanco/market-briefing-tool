Oil (WTI Crude) Briefing — 2026-09-02 📉

WTI closed the week at $84.62, down $2.73 (-3.1%) from $87.35. That's a sizable move on its own, and trailing 8-week vol is running high at 6.76%, so this isn't out of character for the current regime.

Inventories drew down 4.45mm bbls to 424.46mm, versus an 8-week average build of ~2.57mm - a surprise of about -7mm bbls relative to recent trend. On paper that's a bullish inventory signal (tighter supply), which sits awkwardly next to the price drop.

Headlines this week reference Iran escalation risk and Brent pushing toward $95, both of which would typically support higher prices, not a 3% WTI decline. We can't reconcile the headline tone with the price action from this data alone - possible explanations include demand-side concerns, a WTI-Brent spread move, or profit-taking after a prior rally, but none of that is confirmed here. Flagging the disconnect rather than guessing at a driver.

Watch item: check whether the WTI-Brent spread widened this week, which would help clarify if this is a WTI-specific move or a broader crude repricing.
