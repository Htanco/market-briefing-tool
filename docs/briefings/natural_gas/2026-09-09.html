Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-09-09 📉

Henry Hub settled the week at $2.81/MMBtu, down $0.05 (-1.75%) from $2.86. Move was modest and in line with recent trailing volatility (8-week stdev 5.74%), so nothing statistically unusual here.

Storage build was 30 Bcf, slightly below the 8-week average pace of 32.8 Bcf (surprise of -2.8 Bcf). That's a minor undershoot, not enough to call a meaningful tightening signal - stocks still sit at 3,214 Bcf, comfortably above typical seasonal norms heading into fall.

Headline flow this week leans bearish/neutral for price: FXEmpire's oversupply framing and the Investing.com piece flagging record 2026 supply and demand both point to ample balances persisting, consistent with the softer print. The Yamalo-Nenets drone attack on a Russian gas processing plant is a supply-side risk headline but has no clear read-through to Henry Hub fundamentals - worth noting but not a price driver here. The LNG bunker fleet story is longer-horizon demand context, not a near-term factor.

Watch item: next week's storage report for confirmation of whether the sub-average build was noise or the start of a pattern as we approach the end of injection season.
