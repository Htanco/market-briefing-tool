Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-10-07 📈

Henry Hub ticked up to $3.05 from $3.02, basically flat on the week (+1%). Not much signal in that move given weekly vol has been running around 3.3%.

Storage is the more interesting data point. Build came in at +64 Bcf, well above the trailing 8-week average of +33.4 Bcf — a 30.6 Bcf positive surprise. That's a big injection for this time of year, and stocks now sit at 3,415 Bcf. Normally a build that far above trend would pressure prices lower, but price actually firmed slightly, which lines up with the "rallying in peak shoulder season" headline — suggests demand (LNG feedgas, early cold-weather positioning, or storage-economics buying ahead of winter) is offsetting the bearish storage read. The New Fortress Energy headline is a single-counterparty payment dispute, not a Henry Hub supply/demand driver, so no direct read-through to the US benchmark. The Maple Ridge leak is a local safety/operational item, not a market mover.

Net: price stable, storage build large and bearish on paper, but market shrugging it off. Watch next week's injection data to see if the shoulder-season rally has legs or if storage surprises start to weigh.
