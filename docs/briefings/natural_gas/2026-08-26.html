Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-08-26 📈

Henry Hub added 2.5% this week, settling at $2.86 from $2.79, continuing a firmer tone that one technical piece flagged as a break above key resistance - consistent with the price action here, though we'd treat that as chart commentary rather than fundamental signal.

The more relevant driver is the storage build: only +16 Bcf versus an 8-week average pace of +49.2 Bcf, a 33.2 Bcf undershoot. That's a meaningfully smaller injection than recent weeks, and tighter-than-trend builds this time of year typically support price - which lines up with the move higher. Stocks stand at 3,169 Bcf. Volatility remains elevated at 6.17% (trailing 8-week stdev of weekly changes), so this could still be noise rather than a trend shift.

Headlines this week are mostly geopolitical/policy noise - Canada-U.S. trade tension around gas exports, LNG terminal capacity disputes in India, and provincial moratorium reviews in New Brunswick - none of which we can tie directly to this week's domestic storage or price move with confidence.

Watch item: next week's storage report - a second consecutive sub-average build would suggest a genuine tightening trend rather than a one-off draw-down anomaly.
