Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-08-18

Henry Hub added 4 cents this week to settle at $2.66, up 1.5%. Modest move, consistent with the trailing volatility reading of 5.67% weekly stdev — nothing out of the ordinary here.

Storage build came in at 36 Bcf, well below the 8-week average pace of 53.9 Bcf, a negative surprise of about 18 Bcf. That's a second straight week of smaller-than-trend injections, which is mildly supportive for price even with stocks at a comfortable 3,153 Bcf. Not enough on its own to explain the full move, but directionally consistent with the modest uptick.

Headline flow is scattered and mostly not price-moving at the national level: the Explorer Pipeline tank fires and Whittier outage are localized supply/distribution disruptions, and the Southeast cash price plunge suggests some regional basis weakness that's not showing up in the Henry Hub print. On the demand/infrastructure side, the Cameron Parish LNG land story and Targa's new Permian processing deals with Exxon point to continued buildout on both the export and gathering/processing sides, but these are long-cycle items, not this week's price driver.

Watch item: next week's injection data — a third consecutive below-trend build would start to matter more for the strip heading into shoulder season.
