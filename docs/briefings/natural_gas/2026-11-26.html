Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-11-26 📈

Henry Hub spot up 6.6% on the week to $3.23, prior $3.03. Move lines up with this week's storage print: a 24 Bcf draw, the season's first, versus an 8-week average build of +66.4 Bcf — a swing of roughly -90 Bcf relative to recent trend. Headlines confirm the draw came in larger than expected and mark the seasonal turn from injection to withdrawal, which is the straightforward driver behind the bid. Worth noting the bullish storage read is fighting an undertow of weak near-term weather forecasts — a couple of headlines flag forwards getting hit even as spot firmed, suggesting the curve isn't fully buying the rally and traders are discounting mild demand ahead. EQT's bounce on LNG-linked flow adds a secondary, structural support narrative but isn't the main story this week. Vol stayed elevated at 8.37% (8-week stdev of weekly % changes), consistent with a market getting pulled between a tightening storage signal and soft weather. Watch next week's storage print and any firming (or further softening) in December weather forecasts — that will decide whether spot strength holds or forwards drag it back down.
