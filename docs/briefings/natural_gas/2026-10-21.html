Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-10-21 📈

Henry Hub settled the week at $3.20/MMBtu, up 3.2% from $3.10 the prior week. That's a modest move, roughly in line with recent vol (trailing 8-week stdev of weekly changes is 3.07%), so nothing here looks like a dislocation.

Inventories added 67 Bcf, well above the trailing 8-week average build of 43.4 Bcf — a positive surprise of +23.6 Bcf. Stocks now sit at 3,567 Bcf. Normally a bigger-than-expected build would be bearish for price, so the fact that price rose alongside a fat storage injection is worth noting - suggests demand-side or weather-related support is outweighing the supply/storage signal this week, though we don't have enough here to pin down the driver.

Headlines this week are mostly noise for Henry Hub specifically - local pipeline/generation project items (Duke Energy, BrightNight, Alaska pipeline debate) and an insider stock sale at Northwest Natural don't tell us anything about near-term supply/demand balance.

Watch item: next week's storage print, to see if the build pace reverts toward the 8-week average or if the surprise persists as we move into shoulder-season injection flows.
