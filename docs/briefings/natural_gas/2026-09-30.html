Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-09-30 📈

Henry Hub settled at $3.02, up 2.7% w/w, with realized vol still elevated at 3.26% (8-wk stdev of weekly returns). Price action is a bit at odds with the storage print: the build came in at +53 Bcf, well above the 8-week average of +30.2 Bcf (+22.8 Bcf surprise), which on its own reads bearish. That's a notable disconnect worth flagging to the desk rather than explaining away.
