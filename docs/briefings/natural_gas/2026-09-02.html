Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-09-02 📉

Henry Hub eased to $2.81, down 1.75% w/w, staying in the sub-$3 range it's held for a few weeks now. Volatility remains elevated at 5.74% (8-week stdev), so the modest headline move masks some intraweek chop.

Storage added 15 Bcf, well under the 8-week average build of 41.8 Bcf — a 26.8 Bcf undershoot. That's a meaningfully smaller injection than the recent pace, consistent with the "Futures Advance Ahead of Expected Lean Storage Build" headline; the market appears to have priced in the lighter build ahead of the print, which may explain why price didn't rally harder on the tighter number. Stocks now sit at 3,184 Bcf. The FXEmpire piece on consolidation below $3 also lines up with the flat-to-down price action this week — no breakout either direction.

Record open interest at CME is worth flagging separately; it suggests growing positioning/hedging activity into this stalled range, which could amplify the next directional move once it comes. The European storage story is a longer-term demand/LNG consideration, not a near-term driver here.

Watch: whether the next 1-2 storage reports continue running below the 8-week average pace, which would tighten the y/y surplus and could support a break above $3.
