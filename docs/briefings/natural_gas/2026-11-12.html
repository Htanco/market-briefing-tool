Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-11-12 📉

Henry Hub dropped sharply this week, settling at $2.65 from $3.02, down 12.25%. That's a big one-week move and consistent with the elevated vol backdrop - trailing 8-week stdev of weekly % changes sits at 5.87%, so this print is roughly 2x normal weekly swings.

Inventory build came in at 77 Bcf, above the trailing 8-week average build of 63.8 Bcf (+13.2 Bcf surprise). Stocks now at 3,801 Bcf. A bigger-than-trend build into month-end October, with shoulder-season demand still soft, lines up with the price weakness - storage is filling faster than the recent pace, which pressures spot.

Headlines this week are mostly structural/long-term supply and demand-growth stories (Alaska LNG proposal, Daqing output records, Anthropic's gas-generator bet, Woodside on US LNG) rather than anything that explains a near-term demand or weather shock. None of these plausibly drive a 12% weekly price move - treat the drop as fundamentals-driven (large build, soft shoulder demand) rather than headline-driven.

Watch item: next week's storage report - if builds stay above the 8-week average heading into November injection-season close-out, expect continued downside pressure on prompt prices heading into withdrawal season.
