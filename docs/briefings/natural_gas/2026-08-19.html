Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-08-19

Henry Hub added 4.9% this week, settling at $2.79 from $2.66. Move sits within the recent volatility band (8-week stdev ~6.2%), so nothing extreme, but it's a firm up-week rather than noise.

Inventory build was 36 Bcf, below the trailing 8-week average pace of ~53.9 Bcf — a negative surprise of ~18 Bcf. Storage at 3,153 Bcf still comfortable, but the smaller-than-usual injection is consistent with the price bump; tighter weekly balances tend to support spot pricing even with storage well-supplied heading into fall.

Headlines this week are mostly regional/regulatory noise (NV Energy rate filings, Mozambique LNG loan litigation) and don't offer a clean read-through to Henry Hub fundamentals. The Western Canada softness item is worth flagging as a basis/regional divergence story but not a direct driver of the HH move — no direct linkage should be assumed.

Net take: price strength this week looks more inventory-driven (smaller build) than headline-driven. Watch next week's EIA injection number — if the below-average build pace continues into cooler shoulder-season demand, expect follow-through support in the futures curve.
