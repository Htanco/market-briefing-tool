Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-09-23 📈

Henry Hub added 5.4% this week, closing at $2.94 from $2.79, with an 8-week realized vol of ~4.8% suggesting this move is within recent norms, if on the stronger side. Notably, the price gain came despite a larger-than-expected storage build: inventories rose 44 Bcf versus an 8-week average build of 28.8 Bcf, a positive surprise of 15.2 Bcf. That combination — bigger injection but higher price — points to demand-side or positioning factors outweighing the bearish storage print, though the headlines don't give us a clean causal story. "Rally Fades, but Natural Gas Futures Cling to $3" suggests the move had more momentum mid-week before settling near $2.94, consistent with some profit-taking. The data-center/power-demand headline is worth flagging as a structural demand theme but isn't tied to this week's move with any precision. Stocks at 3,298 Bcf remain healthy heading into shoulder-season, so the price strength looks more sentiment/positioning driven than fundamentals-driven for now. Watch item: next week's storage report — if builds continue running above the 8-week average while prices hold near $3, that divergence will need scrutiny.
