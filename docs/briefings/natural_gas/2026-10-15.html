Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-10-15 📈

Henry Hub basically flat on the week, up 3c to $3.05, within normal noise given the 3.3% trailing vol. Not much to read into on price action itself.

Inventory is the more interesting data point. Build of 85 Bcf vs. an 8-week average build of ~37 Bcf — a 48 Bcf positive surprise. That's a big injection relative to recent pace, pointing to softer demand or stronger supply than the recent trend. Stocks now at 3,500 Bcf. Normally a surprise of this size would put some downward pressure on price, but spot barely moved, so the market may already be pricing in shoulder-season length ahead of winter, or the build was offset by other factors we're not seeing in this data alone.

On headlines: nothing directly ties to this week's number. The AI data center power demand story is a structural long-term demand theme (more gas-fired generation potentially needed), not a near-term inventory driver — worth tracking but not a read on this week's build.

Watch item: next week's injection pace — confirm whether the 85 Bcf build was a one-off or the start of a trend, which would matter for winter storage adequacy.
