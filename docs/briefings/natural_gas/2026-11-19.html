Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-11-19 📈

Henry Hub jumped 16.1% on the week, from $2.61 to $3.03, a big move even against an already-elevated vol backdrop (trailing 8-week stdev of weekly changes at 8.1%). Storage added 61 Bcf, bringing total stocks to 3,862 Bcf, but that build came in 7.4 Bcf below the recent 8-week average pace of 68.4 Bcf — a modestly bullish inventory signal (smaller build than trend) that aligns directionally with the price strength, though it doesn't explain a move of this size on its own.

On news flow, the Venture Global/China Gas long-term LNG deal (1M tonnes/year) adds to the demand-side narrative for US gas exports, and is a plausible contributor to sentiment, though a single new offtake agreement of that size is unlikely to move spot Henry Hub materially by itself. The FXEmpire piece flagging a possible breakout above $3.30 suggests technical traders are watching this level closely following the rally. No clear connection between the DTE rate-case headline, the Richards Bay court challenge, or the graphite plant story and this week's price action — treating those as background noise for now.

Watch: whether next week's storage build continues to undershoot the 8-week average, which would reinforce the tightening narrative behind this rally.
