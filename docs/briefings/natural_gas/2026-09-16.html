Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-09-16 📉

Henry Hub slipped 4.1% this week to $2.79, down from $2.91, consistent with the "Bulls Stumble" headline noting failure to clear the $3 level again. That resistance is starting to look sticky — this is at least the second attempt this cycle that's faded.

Storage build came in at +40 Bcf, above the 8-week average of +28.9 Bcf, a surprise of +11.1 Bcf. That's a bearish print relative to recent pace and lines up with the price weakness — supply/demand balance looks a bit looser than the trailing trend suggested, which gives the market less reason to chase $3.

Vol stayed moderate at 4.5% (8-week stdev of weekly changes), so this wasn't a disorderly move, more a grind lower on soft fundamentals. Other headlines this week (LNG project news, unrelated local incidents) don't have a clean read-through to price or storage — nothing there worth building a thesis on.

Watch item: next week's storage report — another above-average build would reinforce the pattern of easing tightness and could keep price capped well under $3 into shoulder-season demand lull.
