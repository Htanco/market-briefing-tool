Natural Gas (Henry Hub) Briefing — 2026-10-28 📉

Henry Hub fell 5.6% on the week to $3.02, down from $3.20, with trailing 8-week volatility at 3.86% - so this move is a bit sharper than the recent norm but not extreme. The inventory build of 77 Bcf came in well above the 8-week average pace (49.8 Bcf), a surprise of +27.2 Bcf, pointing to looser balances heading into shoulder-season demand. That build is consistent with the price weakness, though the headline flow is mixed and largely unrelated to the fundamentals driving this week's number - the Camden County leak stories are a local safety/infrastructure item, not a supply-demand signal, and the EQT/LNG expansion and PHMSA regulatory items are longer-term structural stories rather than weekly drivers. One headline cuts against the bearish price action: reports of "cooler forecasts and restored LNG shipments" pushing prices higher don't line up with the realized weekly decline, so either that catalyst faded, was already priced in by week's end, or affected only part of the window. Worth flagging the disconnect rather than forcing a narrative. Watch next week's storage report for confirmation of whether the oversized build was a one-off or the start of a trend into the fall injection season's final stretch.
