Wheat (Kansas City HRW) Briefing — 2026-09-07

KC HRW cash bid fell 4.4% on the week to $8.89/bu from $9.30, a fairly sharp move — trailing 8-week vol sits at ~5.0%, so this isn't out of line with recent swings but it's on the larger side of the range. No clear single driver in the headline flow; this week's items are mostly agronomic/policy noise (sawfly biocontrol research, Pakistan farmer pricing disputes) or unrelated to price formation, so we're not forcing a narrative here — treat the drop as a plain price move until we see something more concrete.

On the export side, national all-wheat-classes net sales for the week ending 8/27 came in at 313,516 mt, essentially in line with the trailing 8-week average (308,991 mt) — a modest ~4,500 mt positive surprise, not meaningful. Sales pace is running steady, not accelerating or falling off. Worth flagging that this data lags the price week by about a week and covers all wheat classes nationally, so it's not a direct read on KC HRW cash specifically.

Watch item: next week's export sales report (covering the week including this price drop) for any pickup or falloff in pace that might help explain whether the cash decline reflects demand softening or is purely technical/basis-driven.
