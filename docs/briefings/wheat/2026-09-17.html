Wheat (Kansas City HRW) Briefing — 2026-09-17 📉

Quick wheat note – week ending 9/17

KC HRW cash bid drifted to $8.81/bu, down $0.04 (-0.45%) on the week, essentially flat. Intraweek action was choppy per the headlines (early losses Thursday, then a partial bounce), consistent with the trailing 8-week volatility reading of 4.59% — this market's still chopping around without a clear directional trend.

On the export side, national all-wheat-classes net sales for the week ending 9/10 came in at 325,935 MT, about 22,300 MT above the trailing 8-week average of 303,678 MT. Pace is running modestly ahead of recent norms, not a blowout number, but a mild positive signal for demand. Worth flagging this is a national sales flow across all wheat classes, so it doesn't map directly onto the KC HRW cash move.

Headlines were mixed and mostly noise for our purposes — day-to-day price chatter and a China-demand-driven grains wrap (corn/soy/wheat complex, not HRW-specific), plus a Pakistan stocks/price item that's tangential to the US cash market. Nothing there gives a clean causal read on this week's price or sales figures.

Watch item: next week's ESR print to see if the sales-pace uptick continues or was a one-off.
